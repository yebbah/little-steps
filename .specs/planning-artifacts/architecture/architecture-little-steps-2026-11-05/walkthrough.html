How LittleSteps is put together

A guided tour of the architecture spine for the first release. It covers how the parts fit, which rules keep separately built pieces consistent, and what is still open. The spine itself, ARCHITECTURE-SPINE.md, is the binding source; this page explains it.

One Spring Boot app, 15 modules React PWA, offline-first 24 decisions (AD-1 to AD-24) Runs fully on a Mac ≤ US$5 per family per month
1 · The shape

One app, a few containers, a model on the side

A Caregiver's phone, tablet or laptop runs the PWA. It talks to a single Spring Boot application, which serves both the app and its API. PostgreSQL, Keycloak and file storage sit beside it. The AI model runs natively on the Mac during development and is swapped for a hosted model at launch by configuration alone. The same compose file runs locally and on a small server.

    4 · AI safety

    Every AI answer passes the same five gates

    No feature module talks to a model directly. Each use is a named AI task in the ai module, and its output is checked before anything reaches a Caregiver (AD-10 to AD-14). If a check can't run, the item is dropped. The exception is a therapist's prescribed Activity: it is shown with a warning, never hidden.

    1. Allow-listed inputOnly the nickname or "the child", plus Goal, Strategy and Observation content. No field for identifiers exists.
    2. Masked documentsImports reach AI only after the Caregiver confirms the masking. Originals never enter ai.
    3. Schema checkOutput must be structured and valid. No schema has a Goal-status field.
    4. Restriction + scope checkA deterministic match against every Restriction and "avoid" sensitivity, then a scope label: "check with your therapist".
    5. CitationsEach progress or Summary sentence must cite Observations in scope, or it is dropped.

    Budget and speed: Coach Tips wait at most 1.5 seconds for AI before showing curated tips only. A per-family monthly counter switches AI off above a soft cap, so cost stays under the US$5 ceiling.

    5 · Privacy

    Family data is fenced in, logged without content, and fully deletable

    Tenant fence

    Every family-owned row carries family_id, filtered automatically by Hibernate. The family comes from the signed-in Caregiver, never from the request (AD-2).

    No content in logs

    Caregiver and AI text live in value types that print as [redacted]. Logs carry IDs, enums and counts only (AD-19).

    History, not overwrites

    Edits add revisions. Two Caregivers editing at once both keep their version, shown in History (AD-6).

    Deletion reaches every copy

    Seven days to undo, then purge: database rows, stored files, Keycloak accounts, and backups that age out within 14 days (AD-16).
