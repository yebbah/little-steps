# little-steps - Requirements Inventory

Reference list for all epics. Each epic file cites the IDs it covers, and its requirements are confirmed when that epic is approved.

### Functional Requirements

- **FR-1 [P1]:** A Caregiver signs in with their own login (any supported browser, installed or not), creates a Family, and invites other Caregivers.
  - Consent covering storage, AI processing and sharing is required at Family creation and at invite acceptance, and the account is unusable without it.
  - Every Observation and edit records its Caregiver.
  - Invited Caregivers see the same Goals, Plan and Observations.
  - A Caregiver can leave, any Caregiver can remove another, and the remaining Caregivers are notified.
- **FR-2 [P1]:** Caregivers maintain the Child Profile: nickname, optional age band, interests, materials and toys at home, sensitivities, and engagement notes.
  - No real name, date of birth or other identifier is requested.
  - A sensitivity marked "avoid" becomes a Restriction.
  - Suggestions, Coach Tips and the Plan use the Child Profile.
- **FR-3 [P1]:** Caregivers add Care Team Members with a role, a discipline, a Care Setting, their own Status Vocabulary (starting from the default and editable per member) and an optional Prompt Level mapping.
- **FR-4 [P1]:** Caregivers create Goals with a Domain, description, Source, type (Structured or Narrative), optional parent long-term Goal, Criterion (if Structured), Context, Goal Period and optional baseline.
  - Caregiver-entered Goals are parent-proposed with Review Status *proposed* and look visually distinct everywhere.
  - The app prompts for an observable-behaviour description, which the Caregiver can accept or ignore.
  - Editing a Care Team Goal preserves the original wording and shows that a Caregiver edited it.
- **FR-5 [P2]:** Caregivers import a PDF, a photo, a screenshot or pasted text, and get draft Goals, Strategies, Restrictions and prescribed Activities.
  - Likely identifiers are detected and masked, and the Caregiver confirms or adjusts the masking before anything goes to AI. No unmasked identifier is ever sent.
  - The original is kept privately, linked to its extracted items, visible only to the Family, deletable at any time, and deleted automatically after 12 months or on Family deletion.
  - Nothing becomes active until confirmed. Every field is editable before and after confirmation, and uncertain fields are flagged.
  - The original wording is preserved, and any simplification is shown separately.
  - Out of scope: import with no review step, and the iOS share sheet.
- **FR-6 [P1]:** Caregivers start a new Goal Period for a Care Team Member and choose a lineage for each Goal: carried over (keeps its Observations), revised (a new Goal linked to the earlier one, which closes), new, or retired (closed and read-only).
  - Past periods and retired Goals stay viewable with their Observations.
  - Progress follows a revised Goal back through its predecessors.
- **FR-7 [P1]:** Caregivers record Strategies, Restrictions and prescribed Activities and attach them to Goals.
  - They are entered by hand or captured through import.
  - Prescribed Activities appear exactly as written, labelled with their Source, and AI never rewrites them.
- **FR-8 [P1]:** Caregivers browse Activities by Goal, save favourites and hide Activities.
  - Each Activity shows its purpose, linked Goals, materials, time needed (2–10 minutes), steps, variations, Cues, Coach Tips and what to observe.
  - Hidden Activities are excluded from suggestions and the Plan, and favourites get priority.
  - A professionally reviewed starter library covers the initial Domains.
- **FR-9 [P2]:** Caregivers request AI Activity ideas for a Goal, optionally filtered by time, materials or Context.
  - Suggestions use the Goal, its Strategies, the Child Profile, and engagement history for the same Goal or the same Domain and Context.
  - Before display, each suggestion is checked against every Restriction, and one that conflicts is never shown.
  - Suggestions that aren't general engagement techniques are labelled "check with your therapist".
  - Suggestions are labelled *AI-suggested*. Suggestions for *proposed* Goals say the Goal hasn't been discussed with the Care Team yet.
  - The guardrail evaluation set of at least 30 cases must pass before the pilot.
- **FR-10 [P1; Guided mode P2]:** Caregivers prep an Activity, do it with the app out of the foreground, and review afterwards.
  - A Practice Session holds 1–2 Activities.
  - The default flow needs no screen interaction between Prep and Review.
  - The Caregiver can pause, adapt, skip or stop at any time. Stopping leads to the Review and is never presented as a failure.
  - The timer is one tap, optional and never shown as a target.
  - In Guided mode, steps appear one per screen, the screen stays awake, and Marks remain available.
- **FR-11 [P1]:** Before an Activity, Caregivers see a Prep card with the objective, materials, the Care Team Strategy and Prompt Level guidance for the linked Goal, 2–3 Cues, and what to try if it gets hard.
  - It reads in under a minute, opens without starting a session, and can be reopened at any time.
- **FR-12 [P1]:** During an Activity, a Caregiver taps one Mark (Losing attention, Upset, Too easy, Too hard), which is saved with the session.
  - Up to 3 Coach Tips follow.
  - Marking takes one tap, and tips can be dismissed.
  - Tips appear in 2 seconds or less, with curated tips first.
  - The starter tip set covers all 4 Marks for reading, play, meals and movement, and is professionally reviewed.
  - Tips draw on the child's interests and the Care Team Strategies, and are checked against Restrictions.
  - A tip can be marked helpful during the Activity or in the Review, and helpful tips appear first next time.
  - Marks pre-fill the Review.
- **FR-13 [P1]:** Caregivers accept or edit a suggested weekly Plan of 3–5 Activities (adjustable) across active Goals, each optionally placed in a Context or on a day.
  - The Plan is generated from active Goals, favourites, engagement history and the Caregiver's stated availability, and Activities going well get priority.
  - The week starts on Monday by default and is changeable. Unfinished items never roll over.
  - The in-app nudge on Plan days is on by default, at a time chosen in onboarding. Push reminders are opt-in, offered after install, accepted in one tap, and adjustable or switchable off.
  - Reminder wording never mentions missed sessions.
  - Today shows one primary Activity, one tap away.
- **FR-14 [P1]:** Caregivers log an Observation for a Practice Session (the Review) or a spontaneous moment (Log a moment).
  - Fields match the Goal's Criterion (trials, minutes or count), plus Prompt Level and who modelled it, Context, a 3-point engagement scale, which Cue or tip helped, and an optional note.
  - A typical Review takes 30 seconds or less, with elapsed time, attention duration and Marks pre-filled.
  - "Not attempted / child not available" is a neutral entry.
  - Log a moment is one tap from Today. It links to a Goal or an Inventory list and asks only what was seen and in which Context.
  - Every Observation records its Caregiver, Context and Practice Session (if any).
  - Observations can be edited, and the edit history is kept.
- **FR-15 [P2]:** Caregivers keep Inventory lists: new words (tagged by function), new gestures (understood or used), and scripts heard (phrase, Context, likely intent, how it was adapted).
  - They also add tagged notes such as feeding or sensory.
  - Each item records when it was first seen and in which Context.
  - Out of scope: structured feeding programmes and sensory profiles.
- **FR-16 [P1 basic; trends P2]:** Caregivers open a Goal and see its progress across the Goal Period as three separate measures:
  - practice consistency: sessions per week
  - performance: units, Prompt Level trend, and attention duration where recorded
  - generalisation: the Contexts the skill was observed in
  - The view shows the number of Observations.
  - Below 5 Observations it shows the points and says "too few to show a trend".
  - Attention duration is never framed as a target.
  - AI descriptions only describe, cite their Observations, and draw no clinical conclusions.
- **FR-17 [P1]:** Caregivers generate a Summary for one Care Team Member, covering a date range or a Goal Period.
  - For each Goal it shows the three measures, the number of Observations, Prompt Level and Context patterns, new Inventory items, notable moments, and the Caregiver's questions.
  - Parent-proposed Goals are listed separately.
  - It uses one standard format organised by Goal, in the Goal's Criterion units and the member's Status Vocabulary.
  - Goals with fewer than 5 Observations say "limited data".
  - Every statement traces to Observations. AI may suggest questions (labelled), but never a status.
  - The draft is editable, and sharing outside the Family needs confirmation.
  - It exports as a PDF or through the device share sheet.
- **FR-18 [P1]:** After an appointment, Caregivers record Appointment Feedback attached to Goals.
  - It can hold a goal status given by the therapist (in their Status Vocabulary) and change a parent-proposed Goal's Review Status to *discussed* or *confirmed*.
  - New Strategies and Restrictions take effect in the next Plan and in later suggestions.
- **FR-19 [P1, built first] (SPEC CAP-19):** A developer brings the whole product up on a Mac with `deploy/dev-up.sh` and tears it down with `deploy/dev-down.sh`.
  - `dev-up.sh` checks prerequisites (Docker, Java, Node, Ollama and the configured model) with fix steps, creates `local.env` from the example, starts PostgreSQL, Keycloak (realm imported) and Mailpit, and waits for health. It is idempotent.
  - `--full` builds and runs the app image from `deploy/Dockerfile`, and a phone reaches it over Tailscale HTTPS.
  - `dev-down.sh` keeps data, and `--wipe` resets volumes and local files.
  - The README covers first-time setup.

### NonFunctional Requirements

- **NFR-1 (Clinical safety):** AI output is never clinical advice, never a diagnosis, never an effectiveness claim and never a goal status.
  - Care Team Strategies and Restrictions take precedence.
  - Tips and suggestions are limited to general positive engagement and are checked before display.
- **NFR-2 (Content review):** A qualified professional reviews the starter Activities, Cues and Coach Tips before the pilot.
- **NFR-3 (Privacy, data minimisation):** No identifiers are required.
  - AI requests carry only the nickname or "the child" plus the content they need.
  - Uploaded documents are masked before AI.
  - Any hosted AI provider is set not to train on user data.
  - A local model keeps child data on the host.
- **NFR-4 (Access):** Only the Family's Caregivers access its data.
  - Operator staff have none, except time-limited support access granted by a Caregiver (architecture OQ-1).
- **NFR-5 (Audit):** An append-only audit log records sign-ins, membership changes, exports, shares and deletions, and is viewable by the Family.
- **NFR-6 (Encryption):** All data is encrypted in transit and at rest.
  - Originals are kept in private storage.
- **NFR-7 (Data rights):** Caregivers can export all their data.
  - Any Caregiver can delete all Family data, with a typed confirmation, a notice to the other Caregivers, and 7 days to undo before deletion is permanent.
  - When the pilot ends, Families can export, and remaining data is deleted on an announced schedule.
- **NFR-8 (Tone):** Warm, nonjudgmental, caregiver-friendly language.
  - No streaks, scores or "missed" counters.
- **NFR-9 (Platform):** A responsive PWA for phones, tablets and computers, with no native app.
- **NFR-10 (Browsers):** Current Chrome, Safari, Firefox, Edge and Samsung Internet on iOS, iPadOS, Android, macOS and Windows.
  - Every feature works uninstalled.
  - Install is offered where supported, based on feature detection rather than browser sniffing.
- **NFR-11 (Deployment):** The full product, AI included, runs end to end on a Mac with no paid services.
  - Every component is portable to low-cost hosting without code changes.
- **NFR-12 (Speed):** App open to today's Prep card takes 30 seconds or less.
  - Coach Tips appear in 2 seconds or less.
- **NFR-13 (Resilience):** Practice Session data (Marks, timer, Review) survives a dropped connection.
  - Prep cards and starter Coach Tips for the current Plan are available offline.
- **NFR-14 (Accessibility):** WCAG 2.1 AA, large touch targets and system text sizing.
- **NFR-15 (Language):** English in v1.
  - User-facing text is kept separate from code.
- **NFR-16 (Cost):** At most US$5 per Family per month at pilot usage (AI plus hosting).
  - Local development costs nothing beyond the Mac.
- **NFR-17 (Observability):** Structured logs, traces and AI evaluation metrics.
  - Logs never contain child content or identifiers.
- **NFR-18 (Retention):** Uploaded originals are deleted 12 months after upload unless the Caregiver deletes them sooner.
- **NFR-19 (Metrics):** The pilot can measure SM-1 to SM-8 (PRD §9), including the monthly "easier / same / harder" check-in and the therapist-usefulness flag in Appointment Feedback.

- **NFR-20 (Vulnerability gate):** A security scan runs before every commit and again in CI.
  - Any finding in the CISA KEV catalog, or with CVSS High or Critical (7.0 or above), blocks the commit or build until it is fixed.
  - Staged changes are also scanned for secrets.
- **NFR-21 (Hardened images):** Container images come from Docker Hardened Images where the catalog has them, and are pinned by digest.
  - Any image sourced elsewhere is listed as a warning in the release note.
- **NFR-22 (Release notes):** Every completed epic produces a release note covering summary, new features, changes, integration notes, security, code quality, and test results.
  - Test results cover the epic's new positive and negative tests, and the full non-regression suite.

### Additional Requirements

From the architecture spine (AD-n). Items marked **[Epic 1]** are foundation work.

- **[Epic 1] Starters:** Spring Initializr (Spring Boot 4.1.2, Java 25, Maven) for `backend/`, and `npm create vite@latest` with the react-ts template plus vite-plugin-pwa 2.0.0 for `frontend/`. Repo layout: `backend/`, `frontend/`, `content/`, `eval/guardrails/`, `deploy/`. Versions are pinned per the spine Stack table, and Boot 4.2 / Modulith 2.2 / Spring AI 2.1 move together if Boot 4.2 is GA at scaffold time.
- **[Epic 1] Local environment (AD-22, FR-19):** `deploy/Dockerfile` (multi-stage: PWA plus Spring Boot), `deploy/compose.yaml` (PostgreSQL 18.6, Keycloak 26.8.1 with realm import from `deploy/keycloak/`, Mailpit, and Presidio in P2), `local.env.example`, and `dev-up.sh` / `dev-down.sh`. Spring profiles are `local`, `test` and `prod`, with secrets from the environment only.
- **[Epic 1] Module skeleton (AD-1):** Spring Modulith modules `family, child, careteam, goals, library, practice, planning, progress, summary, import, ai, notifications, audit, privacy, metrics`, plus `shared`. The dependency graph is exactly as in the spine, and `ModularityTests` runs `ApplicationModules.verify()` in CI. Cross-module events use `@ApplicationModuleListener` with the JPA event publication registry, ID-only payloads and idempotent listeners. Platform-consumed events are defined in the platform module's `api`.
- **[Epic 1] Tenancy (AD-2):** `family_id NOT NULL REFERENCES families ON DELETE CASCADE` on every Family table, Hibernate `@TenantId`, the resolver taking the Family from the Caregiver's membership, the root tenant for jobs only, native SQL filtering explicitly (test-checked), and `backend/GLOBAL_TABLES.md`.
- **[Epic 1] Auth (AD-3):** Keycloak OIDC public client with PKCE and rotating refresh tokens, Spring OAuth2 resource server, Caregiver keyed by `sub` with a profile upserted from claims, invite-only access (`invite-required`), the `consent-required` gate, `CaregiverSignedIn` audit, and memberships ended rather than deleted.
- **[Epic 1] API contract (AD-20):** `/api/v1` JSON, RFC 9457 problem types from `shared/web/ProblemTypes`, springdoc OpenAPI, and TypeScript types generated with openapi-typescript 7.13.0 run under TypeScript 5.9.3 (the app uses TS 7.0.x). One Flyway history under `db/migration/<module>/` with timestamped, forward-only migrations.
- **[Epic 1] IDs (AD-4):** UUIDv7 everywhere, client-generated for offline-creatable aggregates, with idempotent `PUT` creates. Child rows exist only inside their aggregate's command. Curated content uses UUIDv5 from its slug.
- **[Epic 1] Client data layer (AD-5, AD-15, AD-23):**
  - One Dexie database holding the outbox, drafts and the offline snapshot. The outbox tracks the enqueuing `sub`, coalesces commands, retries FIFO, pauses on 401 for in-place re-sign-in, and parks other errors with Retry.
  - Drafts autosave, and Dexie is wiped on sign-out, user change, membership end or purge.
  - TanStack Query for server reads. The service worker precaches the app shell only. Radix primitives use plain CSS modules on DESIGN.md token CSS variables, and the frontend uses npm.
- **[Epic 1] Security gate (AD-25):** `deploy/security-scan.sh` run by the `.githooks/pre-commit` hook and in CI.
  - gitleaks on staged changes
  - CycloneDX SBOMs scanned by Grype
  - an image scan
  - Blocks on KEV or CVSS 7.0 and above. Exceptions only when no fix exists, logged in `security/exceptions.yaml`, 30 days at most.
  - Actions pinned to SHAs, and scanners pinned and checksum-verified.
- **[Epic 1] Hardened images (AD-26):** a `deploy/images.yaml` manifest, DHI for PostgreSQL and the Node and JRE stages, official-publisher images for Keycloak, Mailpit and Presidio flagged as `other`, everything digest-pinned and CI-checked, and a `dhi.io` login check in `dev-up.sh`.
- **[Epic 1] Release notes (AD-27):** `docs/release-notes/TEMPLATE.md`, plus `deploy/release-notes.sh` gathering commits, migrations, config, security, quality and test facts. One note per completed epic.
- **Revisioning (AD-6):** Goal, Strategy, Restriction, Child Profile, Care Team Member, Observation, Inventory item, Appointment Feedback and Summary draft are append-only revisions with `based_on_revision_id`. Concurrent edits are kept and flagged, and Undo appends a voided revision. Plan items and preferences are set-state. The audit log is insert-only.
- **Practice Session (AD-7):** Client-owned until a single `CompletePracticeSession` command (session, Marks, adapt/skip events, displayed tips, Helpful toggles, Review). The stale-session prompt after 12 hours offers "Review it" or "Close without review".
- **Time (AD-8):** Client `occurred_at` clamped to 14 days, server `recorded_at` and Caregiver, Family IANA timezone and `week_start`, `shared.FamilyCalendar`, a Plan keyed by (family, week start) with `ON CONFLICT DO NOTHING`, and a `week_start` change taking effect from the next week.
- **Ownership table (AD-9):** Prompt Levels and Status Vocabulary in careteam (retire-only, with ordinal snapshot). Goal Periods in goals, with `proposed_to` for parent-proposed Goals. All Activities, links, favourites/hidden (per Family) and ranking in library, fed by events from practice. Inventory in practice, with uniqueness and merge. Plan and reminder prefs (per Caregiver) in planning. Summary and Feedback in summary. Push subscriptions in notifications.
- **Provenance (AD-10):** `source_ref {kind, id}` and `origin ∈ {prescribed, curated, ai_suggested, parent_proposed}`, write-once `original_text` with `caregiver_text`, and prescribed Activities never sent to AI.
- **Guardrail (AD-11):** `RestrictionLookup` (defined by `ai`, implemented by `goals`) combining Restriction rows with live "avoid" sensitivities and a `restriction_set_version`. `Guardrail.check()` runs a deterministic match, then the scope classifier, on every endpoint returning Activities, Plan items, Prep cards or tips. It fails closed, except that prescribed Activities are flagged rather than dropped.
- **AI (AD-12):** Only `ai` uses Spring AI 2.0.2, through named tasks with allow-listed typed inputs and schema-validated outputs. Extraction and summary drafting run as async jobs with cancel. AI tips time out at 1.5 seconds. A per-Family monthly usage counter has a soft cap that falls back to curated content. Ollama locally, a hosted no-training provider in prod.
- **Citations and status (AD-13):** `progress.api.GoalMeasuresQuery` is the only aggregator. AI sentences are cited and immutable, separate from Caregiver blocks. Status is set only through Feedback calling `goals.api`. `ConfirmSummaryShare` freezes an audited snapshot, the PDF (openhtmltopdf 1.1.108) is fetched before sharing, with a download fallback.
- **Import (AD-14, P2):** State machine `uploaded → text_read → masking_confirmed → extracted → reviewed`, `MaskedDocument`-only extraction, `OcrPort` (adapter still open, architecture OQ-3), `MaskingPort` backed by the Presidio 2.2.366 container, encrypted originals with `retain_until`.
- **Offline snapshot (AD-15):** `GET /api/v1/offline-snapshot` (Plan, Prep cards, ranked checked tips, `restriction_set_version`), refreshed on open, focus, coming online and after sync. `navigator.storage.persist()`.
- **Deletion, retention and export (AD-16):** family owns the 7-day deletion and purge, Keycloak user removal through `IdentityPort`, and the tombstone. `pilot_end_at` drives an announcement and then purge. import owns retention purge. privacy owns the async encrypted, expiring, audited export with `ExportContributor` per module. Backups are kept 14 days or less, and purge re-runs after a restore.
- **Jobs (AD-17):** `@Scheduled` and idempotent, with due-column claims and last-run time in actuator health. Reminders: at most one per Plan day, none on non-Plan days, skipped if more than 1 hour overdue. Security notices go through a separate, never-suppressed channel (banner, email, and push if opted in). One app instance.
- **Content (AD-18):** UI copy in frontend message catalogs, with the server returning codes. Curated Activities, Cues and Coach Tips are YAML in `content/` with a slug and `content_version`, loaded after Flyway.
- **Logging (AD-19):** `CaregiverText` and `AiText` print as `[redacted]`. Logs hold IDs, enums and counts only. Prompts and outputs are never logged. `metrics` keeps counts-only `pilot_metric` aggregates.
- **Encryption (AD-21):** `StoragePort` receives ciphertext only (AES-GCM, key ID stored per object), on encrypted disks, with TLS everywhere (Tailscale HTTPS locally).
- **Testing (AD-24):** Testcontainers PostgreSQL (2.x module names) and no H2. The guardrail evaluation set (at least 30 cases) lives in `eval/guardrails/*.yaml` under the JUnit tag `guardrail-eval`, and a pilot release needs a passing report for the configured model.
- **Integrations:** browser web push (VAPID, zerodep-web-push-java 2.1.5), email (Mailpit locally, a transactional provider in prod), the Keycloak admin API (`IdentityPort`), Ollama or a hosted LLM, and S3-compatible storage in prod.

### UX Design Requirements

Sources: `ux-designs/ux-little-steps-2026-11-05/DESIGN.md` and `ux-designs/ux-little-steps-2026-11-05/EXPERIENCE.md` (the spines; they win over the mocks in `ux-designs/ux-little-steps-2026-11-05/mockups/key-*.html`, which were used only to confirm detail).

#### A. Design tokens and foundations

- **UX-DR1:** Implement every light colour token (floor, card, ink, ink-soft, ink-disabled, outline, outline-strong, selected, pressed, tip-surface, scrim, action, action-foreground, block, block-foreground, care-team(-soft), parent-proposed(-soft), danger(-foreground), attention(-soft), mask-cover(-foreground), focus-ring) as CSS custom properties, with the hex values from DESIGN.md frontmatter. [DESIGN › frontmatter colors; DESIGN › Colors]
- **UX-DR2:** Implement a `-dark` twin for every colour token. Dark mode follows `prefers-color-scheme` by default, and a manual Light/Dark/System override in Settings persists per user. mask-cover stays #23252B in both modes. [DESIGN › Colors › Dark mode; EXPERIENCE › Foundation › Theme] (FR-13 Settings)
- **UX-DR3:** Implement the seven Domain tint families (joint-attention, communication, motor-imitation, play, regulation, sensory, other), each in light and dark. [DESIGN › Colors › Domain tints] (FR-4, FR-8, FR-11)
- **UX-DR4:** Implement the Domain → tint mapping function as a unit-tested pure function. It does case-insensitive whole-word keyword matching against the app-held keyword list; the longest matching keyword wins, ties go to the family higher in the table, and no match gives `domain-other`. Test cases: "social interaction and play" → joint-attention, "early motor and sensory skills" → motor-imitation, "oral-motor" → sensory. [DESIGN › Colors › Domain tints] (FR-4)
- **UX-DR5:** An Activity that serves several Goals takes the tint of the first linked Goal's Domain. The tint is applied only to the Today card band, Prep hero, Activity detail hero, Cue tiles and Plan/Activity-row square, and always appears together with the Domain's text label. Tints are never used for status, charts, print or decoration. [EXPERIENCE › Component Patterns › Domain tint; DESIGN › Do's and Don'ts] (FR-8, FR-11)
- **UX-DR6:** Enforce the contrast rules. Every text pairing meets WCAG AA in both modes. Interactive control edges use outline-strong (≥3:1), never `outline`, which is decorative only. Add an automated contrast check against the token pairs listed in DESIGN.md. [DESIGN › Colors › Contrast; DESIGN › Elevation & Depth]
- **UX-DR7:** Colour-usage rules: indigo `action` is the only strong colour on any screen. `selected` is used only for chosen states (always with a check glyph) and `pressed` only for the momentary press fill. No gradients and no traffic-light colours. `danger` is used only for destructive confirms. [DESIGN › Colors; DESIGN › Do's and Don'ts]
- **UX-DR8:** Implement the type scale tokens (display 2.25rem/800, title 1.5rem/800, heading 1.25rem/800, section-label 0.8125rem/700 letter-spaced and never all-caps, body 1.0625rem/400, body-sm 0.875rem, cue 1.1875rem/600, label 0.9375rem/700, meta 0.8125rem/400) in rem on the system font stack, with no web-font download. [DESIGN › frontmatter typography; DESIGN › Typography]
- **UX-DR9:** Root text sizing: `html { font-size: 100% }`, never px. On iOS/iPadOS only, apply `@supports (font: -apple-system-body) and (-webkit-touch-callout: none) { html { font: -apple-system-body } }`, then reset font-family on body. Acceptance: macOS Safari renders body at 17px at default zoom. If the guard misfires on iPadOS desktop-class Safari, fall back to a script check for touch WebKit. [DESIGN › Typography › Text sizing; EXPERIENCE › Responsive & Platform › iOS]
- **UX-DR10:** Implement the spacing tokens (4/8/12/16/24/32/48, gutter 16, margins 16/24/32 for phone/tablet/laptop, content-max 720px, target-min 48px, button-height 52px, mark-target 72px, outline 1px, focus 2px + 2px offset, nav indicator 32×4px). Cards in a group sit 8–12px apart; groups sit 24–28px apart. [DESIGN › frontmatter spacing; DESIGN › Layout & Spacing]
- **UX-DR11:** Implement the radius tokens: xl 28px for cards, hero bands and sheets; lg 20px for block cards, Cue tiles, Marks, tip blocks, icon tiles and Plan rows; md 16px for steppers, fields, status line, banners and Cue numbers; sm 12px for mask covers and the check-tag; full for buttons, chips, badges and the nav bar. No perfect circles for surfaces. [DESIGN › Shapes]
- **UX-DR12:** Flat everywhere. There are no box-shadows on any product element (lint rule or CSS audit). #### I. Accessibility

- **UX-DR116:** Single status region: one `role="status"` region in the app shell, outside routed content, present and empty from first load. No route renders its own. - **UX-DR117:** Focus management on navigation: every route change sets `document.title` ("<Screen>: <subject> · LittleSteps", e.g. "Prep: Storybook together · LittleSteps") and moves focus to the `<h1>`. Done → Review focuses "How did it go?". [EXPERIENCE › Accessibility Floor › Focus management]
- **UX-DR118:** Keyboard and landmarks: full keyboard operation, Tab order follows reading order, and Esc closes the topmost sheet or dialog. A "Skip to content" link precedes the nav, with `<nav>` and `<main>` landmarks. [EXPERIENCE › Accessibility Floor › Keyboard]
- **UX-DR119:** Per-component ARIA conformance: implement and test every row of the Accessibility Floor semantics table (Mark button, removable Mark chip, pre-filled row, single/multi-select chips, toggles, stepper, text field, disclosure rows, sheets/dialogs, status line, banner, timer, Plan row, Today card, Activity row, nav, masking list, destructive confirm, charts) with automated axe checks plus VoiceOver, TalkBack and NVDA passes. [EXPERIENCE › Accessibility Floor table]
- **UX-DR120:** Text size and reflow: content reflows with no truncation or horizontal scroll at 320 CSS px and at 200% text. Test with iOS Larger Text at maximum, Android font scale 200%, desktop browser font settings, and macOS Safari at default zoom (body 17px). [EXPERIENCE › Accessibility Floor › Text size; DESIGN › Typography]
- **UX-DR121:** Reflow rules. Two-button footers (Prep, Review, dialogs) sit side by side only while the container is ≥400px and text scale is ≤130% (container query); otherwise they stack full width with the primary at the bottom. Labels wrap, never `nowrap`. Stepper labels wrap above the control. The Mark grid becomes one column above 130% text or under 340px. At heights under 500px, pinned footers and the bottom nav unpin. `scroll-padding-bottom` equals the pinned footer height. - **UX-DR123:** Never colour alone, plus one-handed reach. Source, AI, status, selection, the active nav item, the next Plan row and Marks always carry text or a shape. - **UX-DR124:** Time limits and orientation: no time limits (Practice never times out, and the 7-day undo is the only time-bound element). Orientation is never locked. Every text field accepts device dictation, and the app records no audio. [EXPERIENCE › Accessibility Floor]
